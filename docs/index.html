Where the evidence has limits

Five reported oxygen values can be associated with community references. Three canal-relative readings remain unmapped because their exact locations are unknown. Rainfall and historical tree-cover loss add geographic context; they do not identify a responsible operation.

Click a community marker for its source. In the collapsed layer menu, choose one rainfall week at a time. Hollow circles mean no value in this source, not zero. Settlement references are not sampling stations.
